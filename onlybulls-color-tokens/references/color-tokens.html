The new palette, applied to the CDS token contract

Every CDS canonical color token, bound to a value from the new palette. This is what goes into ThemeProvider; once set, every CDS component picks it up. Bind the semantic layer, never the primitives.

Contrast ratios on this page are computed in the browser from the values below, so they cannot drift from the swatches next to them. The page renders in its own palette — what you see is what the tokens do.

Contrast matrix

Every foreground against every surface. AA body 4.5:1 · AA large and UI 3:1 · AAA 7:1.

fgDisabled fails everywhere by design — disabled states are exempt from minimums under WCAG 1.4.3. One pair lands short: fgSubtle on bgTertiary at 4.49:1, a hair under AA body. Use it for large text and metadata only, or step up to fgMuted inside cards. bgElevation2 is measured at its composite value #112424, since contrast can't be computed from a translucent layer.

Open decisions

Not mine to close. Everything else above is safe to build on.

Not in the theme

  • Pressed state. #9DDF71 has no CDS token, so it is out of this file entirely. The CTA currently has hover and focus but no pressed value.
  • Gradients, icon sizes, shadows. Removed with the rest of ThemeVarsExtended. They still exist in the Figma spec and will need somewhere to live before build.
  • Purple accent. The palette has no purple; accentBoldPurple / accentSubtlePurple stay on CDS defaults for staking and NFT.

Needs a decision

  • Surfaces are neutral, accents are green. The ramp runs on green-biased neutrals, not the green ramp. Green now appears only at bgHover and bgAlternate — which means hover is the one place a surface shifts hue rather than value.
  • Depth is nearly flat. bgSecondary to bgTertiary is 1.41:1 and bgTertiary to bgElevation2 is 1.34:1. Legible on a good display, easy to lose on OLED at low brightness. Borders may have to carry the hierarchy instead.
  • Card layer moves. Cards were bgSecondary, here they are bgTertiary. Dev renames at call sites.
  • Brand border ≠ brand fill. bgLinePrimary is lime-600 #70B84F while bgPrimary is lime-300. A selected card and a CTA will read as two different greens.
  • Light mode. Dark only. The neutral ramp supports light; the semantic layer does not.

Already drifted

  • Two source tables disagree. In Figma, bgAccent is #7CFF8A in the Color tokens table and #70FF8A in the CDS Token Spec. Neither is used here — this page uses #E8FFD8 for the focus ring — but the Figma pair still needs reconciling.
  • Scrim deepened. bgOverlay moved from 62% to 72%. Anything tuned against the old value needs a look.
